HopHop

A motion game for kids aged 3 to 8. Put the phone on a stand, the camera sees the child, and a character on screen copies their moves to catch snowflakes, stomp apples, pop bubbles and clap mosquitoes.

Camera video is processed on the device only and is never sent anywhere. Details: privacy policy.
